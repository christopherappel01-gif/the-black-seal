QUICK RULES

Risk & Teamwork

Solo: one hero resolves the challenge with one of its listed skills.

Support: one helper may try to add +2. At Threat 3+, Support needs 7+.

Team: major crises use up to three heroes. All roles succeeding gives full success; one missed role usually creates a partial success.

Hope: after a setback, the active hero may spend 2 Hope to turn it into a partial success. At 0 Hope, the company loses that safety net.

Threat: at 3+ support gets harder; at 5+ dangerous challenges are +1 difficulty. At 0 Threat, there is no pressure penalty.

Supplies: setbacks and hard travel can cost Supplies. At 0 Supplies, later strain usually turns into extra Threat or harsher consequences instead.

Intervention: each hero can attempt one Heroic Intervention before a setback is accepted.
